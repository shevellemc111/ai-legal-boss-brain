# AI Legal Boss Brain

The Firm Brain Builder kit for **AI Powered Law Firm Live** (Oct 10, 2026), packaged as one plugin. Every AI employee in this kit runs on a shared set of attorney-only guardrails: no legal advice without review, privileged and confidential by default, conflicts checked before anything else, engagement letters and retainers never sent without explicit approval, a reminder-only operations backstop that is never a docket, and ethics references built for New Jersey and Pennsylvania attorneys.

## Attorney-only guardrails

Every AI employee in this kit reads `references/attorney-rules.md` before doing anything. It covers: no legal advice without review, privileged and confidential treatment of client information by default, a conflict check before every intake (the AI never decides whether a conflict exists — only you do), engagement letters and retainers that never send without your explicit approval of that specific document, an Operations Assistant that is a reminder backstop and never your official docket, and ethics references for New Jersey and Pennsylvania (RPC/Rule 1.5, 1.6, 1.7/1.9, 1.18, 5.3, plus the NJ Supreme Court's January 2024 AI guidance and the PA/Philadelphia Bar's May 2024 Joint Formal Opinion 2024-200). If you're licensed elsewhere, the kit says so plainly and points you to your own state's rules instead of guessing.

Optional team question: if your firm has a team, Operations Assistant setup includes one optional question about whether an AI employee is meant to support or take over tasks from someone already on your team. This documents your plan — it never evaluates or recommends the decision itself.

## How to start

After installing, start a Cowork task, attach the folder you want your Firm Brain files saved in, and say: **"Let's do my firm brain."** Your files are saved in `about-me/` inside that folder, and anything your AI employees draft goes in `outputs/`, in a subfolder for each client or matter.
